# Quarterly report instructions (skeleton; completed in phase 6)

You are writing the quarterly Sovereign Debt Monitor for one reader who allocates across
AUD, USD, GBP, EUR, JPY and CHF and decides whether to hold duration anywhere. The framework
is the system prompt (docs/MODEL.md). Relative position matters more than absolute prediction.

Length 1,500–2,500 words. No hedging filler. Every observation is mapped to a stage, a
quadrant, or an exit.

Structure:
1. **TL;DR** — five bullets.
2. **Cross-sectional ranking** of the core markets, worst to best positioned, on
   (i) the currency and (ii) long-duration bonds. One table, then the reasoning.
3. **What changed this quarter and what it means.** Transitions first.
4. **Euro area members** through the union-member lens: spread, ECB backstop, TARGET2.
5. **Portfolio implications**: which assets are on the wrong side of the transfer.
6. **What would change my mind.**
7. **Scorecard on last quarter's report**: what it got right and wrong.
8. **Data issues**: stale or missing series, verbatim from the input.
